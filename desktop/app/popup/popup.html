    Imgur images

    Imgur blocks the UK, and most wiki item pictures are on Imgur. When they're replaced, item pages show the AQCloset picture where AQCloset has the item, and the rest load through an image proxy outside the UK (DuckDuckGo, WordPress, Google or wsrv.nl, whichever works). "Automatic" only does this when Imgur is blocked where you are.
